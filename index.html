ISRAELI SINGER-SONGWRITER BASED IN TEL AVIV

Through soulful melodies, heartfelt lyrics, and mindful presence, I create music that invites reflection on love, healing, and personal growth. I use songwriting as a tool to explore emotions, celebrate vulnerability, and promote self-discovery.

My songs are born from my open heart, sensitive truth and a mindful openness— to myself, to those around me, and most of all, to the heart. I create from a space of empathy, receptivity, and emotional presence, believing in the power of a song to reflect, connect, and heal.

In recent years, I have been active not only in music but also in the realms of personal and spiritual growth as a yoga instructor in Sri Lanka— a journey that continues to deepen my connection to myself and to what I choose to bring to the forefront of my art. My songs explore pain, tenderness, independence, self-love and love for others, and the courage to walk the path — even when it isn't fully clear.

An independent and powerful artist, I bring to the stage a clear feminine voice — strong, personal, and rooted — with a free and untamed soul. I perform in intimate settings, am involved in new creative projects (a single and an album are on the way), and invite those who listen to simply be — and feel.
